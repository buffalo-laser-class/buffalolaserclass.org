PLACEHOLDER IMAGES
==================

hero.jpg  — The big background photo on the Home page.
            Drop in your real landscape photo of Buffalo with Lasers in the
            foreground here, keeping the filename "hero.jpg". Recommended:
            at least 1600px wide, landscape orientation, saved as a JPG.
            Until a real hero.jpg is present, the Home page shows a navy
            gradient (which looks fine on its own).

favicon.svg — The little icon in the browser tab. Swap for your real logo
              later if you like.

logo (future) — When you have a real logo, see BOARD_GUIDE.md for how to
                replace the "BLCA" text logo in the header.
